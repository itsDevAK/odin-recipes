Fresh Orange Juice

Description

Fresh orange juice takes a little work, but it tastes much better than orange juice from concentrate. I came up with this recipe in response to a request about how to make fresh-squeezed orange juice. You may also use a citrus reamer to do this. If you want less pulp, use a hand juicer with a strainer.

Ingredients

  • 4 oranges

Steps

  1. Combine salt, oregano, pepper, cumin, and cinnamon in a large bowl. Add beef; toss to coat in the spice mixture. Let stand while preparing the sauce mixture, or cover and chill in the refrigerator for up to 24 hours.
  2. Turn on a multi-functional pressure cooker (such as Instant Pot®) and select the Sauté function. Add oil and allow to get hot. Add guajillo chiles; cook and stir until lightly toasted and fragrant, 30 seconds. Add onion; cook until tender, 8 minutes. Add garlic; cook until fragrant, about 1 minute.
