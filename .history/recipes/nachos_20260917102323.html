Beef Birria Nachos

Description

These beef birria nachos are made with tender, mist birria cooked in the Instant pot.

Ingredients

Birria

  • 2 tsp kosher salt
  • 1 tsp dried Mexican oregano
  • 1/2 tsp ground black pepper
  • 1/2 tsp ground cumin
  • 1/4 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 2 pounds boneless beef chuch roast
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 4 dried guajillo chiles
  • 1 cup coarsley chopped onion
  • 3 cloves garlic
