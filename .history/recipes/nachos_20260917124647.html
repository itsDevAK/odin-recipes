Beef Birria Nachos

Description

These beef birria nachos are made with tender, mist birria cooked in the Instant pot.

Ingredients

Birria

  • 2 tsp kosher salt
  • 1 tsp dried Mexican oregano
  • 1/2 tsp ground black pepper
  • 1/2 tsp ground cumin
  • 1/4 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 2 pounds boneless beef chuch roast
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 4 dried guajillo chiles
  • 1 cup coarsley chopped onion
  • 3 cloves garlic
  • 3 cups chicken broth
  • 2 medium tomatoes, chopped
  • 2 tbsp white vinegar
  • 1 tbsp honey

Nachos

  • 6 cups bite-sized tortilla chips
  • 1 (15 ounce) can pinto beans
  • 2 cups shredded Monterey Jack cheese
  • 1/4 cup slivered red onion
  • 1/4 cup sliced pickled jalapeño peppers
  • 1/4 cup crema or sour cream
  • 2 tablespoons chopped fresh cilantro
  • birria cooking liquid, warmed, for serving

Steps

  1. Combine salt, oregano, pepper, cumin, and cinnamon in a large bowl. Add beef; toss to coat in the spice mixture. Let stand while preparing the sauce mixture, or cover and chill in the refrigerator for up to 24 hours.
  2. Turn on a multi-functional pressure cooker (such as Instant Pot®) and select the Sauté function. Add oil and allow to get hot. Add guajillo chiles; cook and stir until lightly toasted and fragrant, 30 seconds. Add onion; cook until tender, 8 minutes. Add garlic; cook until fragrant, about 1 minute.
  3. Add broth and tomatoes. Bring to a boil. Blend until smooth using an immersion blender (or transfer mixture to a blender or food processor; blend until smooth. Return sauce to Instant Pot®).
  4. Add beef, vinegar, and honey to the Instant Pot®. Close and lock the lid. Select High pressure according to the manufacturer’s instructions; set the timer for 45 minutes. Allow up to 20 minutes for pressure to build. Release pressure using the natural-release method according to the manufacturer’s instructions, 10 to 40 minutes. Unlock and remove the lid.
  5. Remove meat from cooking liquid and shred with two forks. Skim fat from cooking liquid; reserve liquid.
